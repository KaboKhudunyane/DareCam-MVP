# DareCam roadmap

## MVP stages
1. **Setup:** Vite TypeScript app, docs, scripts, CI and environment contract.
2. **Design/onboarding:** neon dark design system, landing, 18+ gate, consent.
3. **Identity:** demo player/host roles and profiles; production auth seam.
4. **Live session:** queue, mock camera tiles, session controls and chat.
5. **Card engine:** seeded safe cards, categories, progress, verify/next/skip/redo.
6. **Scoring:** append-only mock ledger, idempotent awards, daily rank/winner.
7. **Rewards:** reward suggestions in chat and host selection placeholder.
8. **Safety:** block/report/leave, card guardrails, consent and moderation view.
9. **Admin:** card management, reports, bans and audit summary in demo console.
10. **Polish:** responsive accessibility, reduced motion, CI and deployment notes.

## Deliberately deferred
Spectator/watch-live and multiple hosts are future add-ins. Payments, real-money rewards, recording, and legal final copy are not MVP features.
